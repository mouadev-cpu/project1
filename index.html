How to import your trades

2 trades a day: 1 Best + 1 Worst

  1. Open Import in the sidebar and drop a CSV file or a screenshot of your trades.
  2. Check the trades we picked. We auto-select your biggest win (Best) and biggest loss (Worst). Want different ones? Choose them yourself and auto-select turns off.
  3. Add your strategy, notes and risk, then press Import.
Write a real Reason For Entry and name your Strategy. Both are optional, but they are what make the Stats page useful. Reasons from all traders are grouped into the daily Summary, and your Strategy shows how many winning traders used the same setup. Write it the way you would explain it to another trader, with trading terms (for example “break of structure, retest of VWAP, long”), not “good setup”. The more specific you are, the better the data for everyone.

You can

  • Import as many trades as you like. Only 2 are kept per day.
  • Replace a saved trade with a new one until 5:55 PM ET (55 minutes after the close on an early-close day). We tell you if the new one is bigger.
  • Import again later the same day.

You can’t

  • Log trades from a past trading day.
  • Use break-even trades (0 points).
  • Keep more than 1 winner and 1 loser per day.
  • Change a trade’s prices or times.
  • Delete a trade while it is on the scoreboard.
Cheer each other on Tap the flame on a winning trade to say well done. Tap the crosshair on a losing trade to tell that trader to lock in and keep going. Double-tap a card to do either.

A trade must open and close while the market is open, on the current trading day. Logging closes at 5:55 PM ET and a new day opens at 6:00 PM ET. On early-close days (the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and July 3) the market closes at 1:15 PM ET, logging closes at 2:10 PM ET and that day’s board opens at 2:15 PM ET.
