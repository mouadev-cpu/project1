Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Nexus (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. Nexus is run by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

The short version: we collect only what we need to run the app, we do not sell your information, we do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and you can delete your account and data yourself at any time in Settings.

1. Information we collect

What you give us

  • Account: email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored in hashed form; we never see it.
  • Profile: username, and optionally a nickname, a short bio and a profile photo or avatar.
  • Trades: the trades you import or save: symbol, direction, size, open and close times, entry and exit prices, risk, results (points, R:R, profit or loss), and any reason for entry or strategy you type.
  • Activity: who you follow, likes you give, people you block, and reports you send (including any note you write), and the notifications generated for you.
  • Settings: theme, time zone and clock format, and whether you have finished the welcome tour.
  • Agreement record: the version of the Terms you accepted and when you accepted it.

Collected automatically

  • Our hosting and sign-in providers receive technical data that comes with every web request, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse.
  • Your browser stores a sign-in session and a copy of your theme on your device so you stay logged in and the page looks right when it opens (see section 8).

What we do not collect: your precise location, your contacts, your brokerage login or account numbers, payment card details, or advertising identifiers.

Trade files and screenshots. A CSV file or screenshot you add to import trades is read on your own device. The file or image itself is not uploaded to us. Only the trade details you review and confirm are saved. Do not worry about other details in the file, such as an account number: we do not save them.

2. What other people can see

Nexus is a social app. Other users can see your username, nickname, bio, profile photo, the trades you save (symbol, direction, times, prices, points and profit or loss amounts), your places on boards, your like counts, and your follower and following counts. Your email address, time zone, settings and blocked list are not shown to other users. Please do not put private or sensitive information in your profile or trade notes.

3. How we use information

  • to create and secure your account and keep you signed in;
  • to run the app: show profiles, boards, rankings, statistics, notifications, follows and likes;
  • to send the messages the service needs, such as the sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails;
  • to review reports, enforce our Terms, and keep the community safe;
  • to fix problems, prevent fraud and abuse, and protect our rights; and
  • to follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you other than the automatic rankings that appear on the boards.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Nexus. They may process your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, user sign-in and file storage (for example profile photos).
  • GitHub: hosts the website files.
  • jsDelivr: a public content network that delivers some open-source code the app uses, so it can see your IP address when that code loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect the safety, rights or property of users or the public, or as part of a sale or transfer of the service (we would tell you if your information became subject to a different policy).

5. Where information is kept and how it is protected

Your information is stored on servers run by our providers, which may be in the United States or other countries. Connections to Nexus are encrypted, access to data is limited by rules in the database itself, and passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. To delete your account: open Nexus, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photo files, trades, follows, likes, blocks, reports you filed, notifications, settings and your agreement record. It cannot be undone. Copies may stay in our providers’ backups for a limited time before they are overwritten. We may keep limited information if the law requires it or if it is needed to resolve a dispute or stop abuse.

If you cannot log in, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can see and edit your profile and trades inside the app.
  • Delete: see section 6.
  • Ask us: to request a copy of your information, a correction, or to object to how it is used, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will answer within the time the law requires.
  • Privacy laws: depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states, the UK or the European Economic Area) you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or move your information and to opt out of sale or targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. You will not be treated worse for using these rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, our reasons for using your information are to provide the service you asked for, our legitimate interests in running and securing it, and your consent where we ask for it; you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Cookies and similar storage

Nexus does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics. It uses your browser’s local storage for two things that are needed for the app to work: your sign-in session, and a copy of your chosen theme so the page does not flash the wrong colors. Clearing your browser data removes them; you will be signed out.

9. Children

Nexus is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
